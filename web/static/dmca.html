DMCA Policy

Trakx is a BitTorrent tracker that only coordinates connections between peers. We do not host, store, or distribute any content.

As a technical limitation, BitTorrent trackers only process info-hashes and cannot determine what content those hashes represent. We have no capability to inspect, filter, or block specific content.

While we respect intellectual property rights, DMCA takedown notices do not apply to our service as we do not host any copyrighted material.
